AEON is a world where every creature thinks with its own tiny neural network — a real brain that mutates and evolves across generations. Nobody is scripted. They learn to survive, gather, breed, band into tribes, and war over land. All of it runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

Scroll zoom — the world is one continuous space from orbit to the ground
Drag pan the world
Left tools shape land, grow forests, spawn life, smite, bless
Click a creature open its brain and lineage
⚑ Nations browse a nation, then Play as to lead it — set its will, research, diplomacy & destiny, or descend into one body
Space pause · 1–5 speed · ☰ menu

The world never pauses for you. Zoom out to feel powerful; zoom in to feel attached.
